Virtual Guard Monitoring, Explained

What is virtual guard monitoring?

Trained agents watch your cameras from our 24/7 Security Operations Center in Marietta. When a person or vehicle is detected, an agent verifies the event, can speak through on-site speakers, and can request police dispatch.

Where is Skynet Monitoring located?

2470 Windy Hill Road, Suite 300, Marietta, GA 30067. The operations center monitors properties in Atlanta and beyond. Call (678) 310-7570 or email sales@skynetmonitoring.com.

How does a virtual guard compare with an on-site guard?

A virtual guard covers the cameras around the clock without a shift change at the property. Plans are priced up to 90% below a staffed post. Coverage still depends on camera placement and the service you choose.

What happens after an alarm is verified?

Analytics set aside animals, weather, and shadows. Verified threats get a live audio warning and, when needed, police dispatch. You receive an incident report and the video.

Which properties can you monitor?

Commercial buildings, construction sites, HOA and multifamily communities, auto dealerships, warehouses, and government facilities. We install new cameras or connect systems you already have.
