Our Services

Comprehensive Virtual Security Solutions

From AI-driven alarm verification to live virtual guard patrols — we deliver proactive protection that prevents crime, not just reports it.

Remote Video Monitoring

Our trained security agents monitor your property via high-definition cameras from our 24/7 Security Operations Center, providing real-time situational awareness around the clock.

AI-Powered Alarm Verification

True AI analytics filter out false alarms caused by animals, weather, and shadows. Only verified threats trigger dispatch — saving you money and reducing unnecessary police calls.

Virtual Guard Tours

Scheduled or on-demand sequential camera patrols of your entire property. Our agents identify threats, verify secure perimeters, and document conditions — just like an on-site guard, remotely.

Live Audio Intervention

Two-way speakers allow our agents to directly address trespassers in real time. The "voice-down" capability creates the illusion of on-site security presence and deters criminals before they act.

Virtual Escort Services

Provide peace of mind for employees closing up at night or property managers showing units. Our agents remotely monitor them via camera as they walk to their vehicles or through your facility.

Remote Access Control

Manage gate, door, and barrier access remotely. Our agents verify identities, grant controlled entry for deliveries and visitors, and maintain a detailed log of all access events.
