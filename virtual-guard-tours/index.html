Structured live camera patrols run by Marietta operators on a fixed schedule — checking doors, gates, lots, and equipment yards with written logs.

Scheduled Patrol Accountability Without Patrol Cars

Traditional roving patrol guards spend a few minutes driving through a parking lot once or twice a night, often missing areas behind buildings or between trailers. A virtual guard tour turns your perimeter cameras into an on-time, documented walkthrough run by an operator inside our Marietta Security Operations Center.

Following your custom tour checklist, an operator inspects each camera zone in sequence — checking whether dock doors closed properly, chain-link gates are latched, lighting is functioning, or unauthorized vehicles remain parked behind retail suites or industrial bays. If someone appears during a patrol, the agent immediately triggers live audio intervention and stays with the camera view.

Included in Every Virtual Tour Program

  • Custom Camera Route Checklist: You specify which gates, dumpster corrals, roof hatches, loading bays, and fuel islands our operators inspect on each pass.
  • Fixed Time Windows or On-Demand Checks: Common Metro Atlanta schedules include post-close verification, mid-night sweeps, and pre-opening readiness checks.
  • Compliance & Maintenance Flags: Beyond trespassing, tours catch propped doors, burnt-out lot lighting, blocked fire lanes, and offline cameras before workday disruptions.
  • Audit-Ready Tour Logs: Management receives regular timestamped summaries confirming each patrol ran as scheduled, along with clips from any exception.

Combining Tours With Continuous Alarm Coverage

While remote video monitoring responds within seconds to active intrusion lines, virtual guard tours verify static conditions that might never trip a motion line — like an unlocked bay door left open by the late shift. Together they deliver stronger coverage across Cobb, Fulton, and Gwinnett properties at a fraction of roving security contracts.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often can virtual guard tours run each night?

Tour frequency is configured to your post orders — from an opening and closing verification sweep to hourly or multi-pass overnight checks.

Can operators report open doors or burned-out lights?

Yes. Tour checklists routinely include checking that warehouse overhead doors are down, entry gates are closed, and site floodlights are operational.

What happens if a patrol uncovers an intruder?

The agent transitions immediately into live intervention mode: addressing the individual over two-way horn speakers and coordinating police dispatch if they remain on site.
