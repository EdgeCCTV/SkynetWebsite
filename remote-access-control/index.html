Stop Leaving Gates Propped Open or Guard Shacks Understaffed

Unmanned gates create two costly problems for Atlanta logistics yards, HOA communities, and commercial properties: either drivers get stuck blocking street lanes for hours, or staff give out static PIN codes that stay active for months after contractors leave. Remote access control puts a live video operator in Marietta on your intercom and barrier gate around the clock.

When a delivery driver, utility tech, or after-hours visitor presses the call button, the audio/video call routes directly to our Security Operations Center. The agent sees the driver and license zone on camera, verifies credentials against your approved vendor schedule or callback rules, opens the gate remotely, and watches on video to confirm tailgating did not occur before the barrier closes.

Gate & Entry Functions Covered 24/7

  • After-Hours Vendor & Freight Entry: Verify bills of lading, technician work orders, or emergency utility dispatches before opening loading gates.
  • HOA & Multifamily Visitor Screening: Manage guest entry during unstaffed gatehouse hours without paying overnight gate-attendant payroll.
  • Anti-Tailgating Audio Deterrence: Immediately warn trailing vehicles via speaker if someone tries to follow an authorized truck through the barrier arm.
  • Searchable Access Audit Trails: Every entry request records who called, what was approved or denied, and linked video clips from the gate cameras.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Skynet control existing lift gates and IP intercoms?

In many cases yes — we inspect your gate controller, relay module, and intercom/camera path during your site assessment to verify compatibility.

How do operators verify unexpected after-hours contractors?

Every property sets strict verification rules in advance: driver photo ID on camera, authorized vendor list, work-order match, or live phone sign-off from an on-call manager.

Is video saved for every gate opening?

Yes. Denied and approved entries are logged with timestamps and paired with camera footage covering the gate lane.
