Stop Trespassers Before Police Even Arrive

Even a fast police response takes several minutes — plenty of time to pry a service door, strip copper wire, or smash truck windows. Live audio intervention closes that gap inside seconds. As soon as our Marietta Security Operations Center verifies an unauthorized person on your lot, the operator keys up the nearest outdoor speaker and speaks directly to them.

These are not pre-recorded robotic loops that repeaters quickly learn to ignore. Our operators address the person by specific details — clothing color, vehicle location, or exact corner of the building — proving a live person is watching in real time. Most trespassers leave the property immediately once challenged by voice.

Strategic Speaker Zones for Commercial Sites

  • Dealership Inventory Rows: Challenge loiterers entering back lots or kneeling beside vehicles after closing hours.
  • Construction Laydown Yards: Warn trespassers approaching tool trailers, copper pallets, and fuel tanks before locks are cut.
  • Loading Docks & Dumpster Pads: Curb illegal dumping, after-hours catalytic converter theft, and rear-door break-in attempts behind shopping centers and warehouses.
  • Multifamily Amenities & Gates: Clear unauthorized groups from pool decks, mailrooms, and entry barriers without confronting residents in person.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are voice-down announcements pre-recorded or spoken live?

They are spoken live by the Marietta SOC operator watching your camera feed, using real details such as location, clothing, or vehicle so intruders know a live guard is watching.

Do I already need speakers installed on our building?

No. If you do not have outdoor IP horn speakers, our Georgia-licensed low-voltage installation team (LVU407024) can add weatherproof PoE speakers during onboarding.

Won't loud speakers disturb neighbors at night?

During site design we orient directional horn speakers into your lot and tune volume profiles by time window so announcements stay clear on your property without blasting neighboring streets.
