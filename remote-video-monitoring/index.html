Proactive Monitoring From Metro Atlanta

Remote video monitoring puts a live operator on your cameras without paying for an unattended guard booth or waiting until morning to pull recorded footage. From our Security Operations Center at 2470 Windy Hill Road in Marietta, GA, trained monitoring specialists watch commercial perimeters, loading docks, gated entries, and parking lots across Atlanta, Buckhead, Sandy Springs, Alpharetta, Kennesaw, and surrounding Georgia corridors.

Unlike passive alarm companies that only react after a door sensor trips, our desk combines analytics with real-time human judgment. When a person or vehicle crosses into a restricted zone after business hours, an agent reviews the live feed immediately, issues a custom talk-down warning through on-site horn speakers, notifies your designated manager, and stays on the cameras while coordinating verified police dispatch if the intruder refuses to leave.
