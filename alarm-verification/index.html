Stop Paying for False Alarm Dispatches

Standard motion detection cannot tell a windblown tree branch, headlights on I-285, rain glare, or a roaming animal from someone cutting a fence lock. When raw motion alerts go straight to central station dispatch or flood managers' phones at 3 a.m., legitimate emergencies get buried under noise — and Atlanta municipalities penalize repeat false dispatches.

Skynet Monitoring places an AI classification layer and a trained human operator in front of every escalation. Object detection identifies human silhouettes and vehicles crossing defined intrusion zones during armed hours, and our Marietta SOC verifies the video clip in real time before anyone picks up the phone to request police response.

What Verified Alarm Triage Delivers

  • Cleaner Escalations: Shadows, insects on IR lenses, rain, and foliage alerts are dropped automatically before reaching dispatch workflows.
  • Visual Priority With Police: Law enforcement responds differently to a live operator reporting a visually confirmed trespasser inside a fenced yard versus an unverified door contact.
  • Documented Event Trails: Managers receive concise incident reports with direct video clips showing why an event was cleared or escalated.
  • Reduced False-Alarm Fines: Because only human-confirmed threats trigger police dispatch, your site avoids nuisance false-alarm citations across Fulton, Cobb, DeKalb, and Gwinnett jurisdictions.

Seamless Pairing With Live Audio & Guard Tours

Verified intrusion monitoring connects directly to live audio intervention so the operator who confirms a person on your loading dock can immediately issue a stern voice-down warning. For quiet periods where you also want proactive proof of perimeter integrity, add scheduled virtual guard tours at closing, midnight, and pre-open.

Do we need new cameras to use AI alarm verification?

Not always. Even when older cameras lack built-in analytics, our platform can apply server-side person and vehicle analytics at the monitoring intake after inspecting stream quality.

Does AI replace human operators?

No. AI handles the first-pass filtering of animals, weather, and headlights. A live operator in our Marietta SOC inspects the alert before speaking on speakers or calling police.

How fast are alarm triggers reviewed?

Our monitoring desk targets under-30-second triage on incoming verified alerts, depending on site connectivity and simultaneous escalation volume.
